How We Use AI

AI-assisted. Human-reviewed.
Never slop.

We use serious AI tools every day, and we're open about it — because the way an agency uses AI tells you almost everything about the work you'll get back. Here is exactly what ours does, and what never happens without a human.

Used Openly• Reviewed by a Human• Never Auto-Published• No Fake Anything

What AI Actually Does Here

Depth and speed a small studio couldn't buy before.

The same class of tools the largest agencies use — applied carefully, and named plainly instead of hidden behind “AI-powered” buzzwords.

Deeper research

AI lets us actually read your whole market — every competitor's site, rankings, reviews, and Google presence — instead of skimming the top three results and calling it an audit.

Faster building

Drafting, coding, and iterating happen in hours instead of weeks. We can test ten versions of a page before most agencies schedule their kickoff call — and you pay for the result, not the meetings.

AI search visibility

We ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini the questions your customers actually ask — several times over — and record how often your business gets named. Then we work on the answer.

Cleaner structure

Sites built the way search engines and AI assistants read them: fast pages, real structured data, and copy that answers the question a machine is trying to answer about you.

Sharper drafts

First drafts of captions, ad copy, and page copy arrive fast — then get rewritten until they sound like your business, not like everyone else's feed.

Honest measurement

More time on tools means more time re-measuring what we changed — rankings, traffic, calls — and showing you the numbers instead of a vibes report.

The Human Part

Nothing ships without a person behind it.

1

AI does the heavy lifting

The sweep, the drafts, the measurement — the work that used to eat the budget before any thinking happened.

2

A human makes the calls

What matters, what's wrong, what fits your business. Judgment and taste don't come from a prompt.

3

Review before release

Every audit, page, post, and email is read by a person before it reaches you or your customers. No exceptions.

Where We Draw the Line

What we will not do with AI.

No auto-published content

We don't point a bot at your blog or social accounts and walk away. Unreviewed AI content is what Google's spam updates exist to bury — and it's your name on it.

No fake reviews or testimonials

We will never generate, seed, or “polish” a review for you or for ourselves. Fabricated reviews are against federal rules, and they're against ours.

No hiding it

We don't run our work through tools to make it “undetectable,” and we decline when asked. An agency willing to disguise how it works for you will disguise other things too.

Why This Matters Now

Your next customer may never see a list of links.

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI answers for a recommendation and go with what comes back. There is no page two of an AI answer — a business is either named or it isn't. Being findable now means being findable to machines that read your website, your reviews, and your Google profile on a customer's behalf.

That is a structural change in how local businesses get discovered, and it rewards exactly the unglamorous work we already do: fast pages, real structured data, complete profiles, consistent information everywhere. We measure where you stand in AI answers today, do the work, and re-measure the same way — so you can see what moved without taking anyone's word for it.

The Honest Part

Why tell you any of this?

Because “AI-powered” has become the most abused phrase in marketing. Some agencies slap it on the same old work; others quietly ship unedited AI output at full price; plenty swear they're “100% human” while doing neither. We'd rather tell you exactly what our tools do and let the work speak.

Used well, AI makes a small studio dangerous: big-agency depth and speed, without the big-agency invoice or the account-manager telephone game. Used lazily, it produces the generic sludge you already scroll past every day. The difference is the human in the loop — and ours is on every deliverable.
